Reassembly lab

You are the destination. Deliver the fragments in any order, lose one, or repeat one, and watch the packet rebuild by byte position.

Tick "lose" on a fragment card to make it vanish in the network. Click a card to deliver it yourself.

Original fragmentable part, by byte position

Waiting for fragments.

An incomplete packet is discarded when the reassembly timer expires (60 s, RFC 8200). If the first fragment had arrived, the destination sends ICMPv6 Time Exceeded (Type 3, Code 1).
