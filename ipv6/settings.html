Settings

Changes apply immediately and are saved in this browser only. Appearance and sidebar settings apply to every IPv6 page.

Appearance

Theme

Light, dark, or follow your device setting.

Accent colour

The colour used for buttons, highlights and the simulation stage.

Text size

Scales all text and spacing on the IPv6 pages.

High contrast

Stronger borders and darker secondary text for easier reading.

Reduce motion

Turns off animations and makes the simulation play quickly. System follows your device.

Sidebar

Sidebar mode

Expanded shows labels, Compact shows only the page numbers, Hidden gives the page the full width. Press B to hide or show it.

Remember collapsed menu groups

Keep the groups you close in the menu closed on the next visit.

Show course card

The course name card at the top of the sidebar.

Show voice assistant

The IPv6 voice assistant panel at the bottom of the sidebar.

Menu groups

Open every group in the sidebar menu.

Simulation

Default playback speed

Used when the simulation opens. Changing the speed on the simulation page updates this too.

Start automatically

Pause when the tab is hidden

Stops the animation while you are on another tab, so you do not miss stages.

Keyboard shortcuts

Space, arrow keys, R, B, D, / and number keys for pages.

Identification format

How the 32-bit Fragment Identification is shown.
